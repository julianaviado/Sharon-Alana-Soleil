Why it feels this way

These aren’t your personality.
They’re patterns.

We’ve lived under pressure so long that taking care of everybody else became second nature, and taking care of ourselves doesn’t come as easily, or at all. You think it’s just who you are. It isn’t. That’s the part that changes everything: patterns can be shed.

Meet Sharon

The story behind the Freedom to Flow Method™

Sharon is the go-to, do-it-all one her whole family leans on, her parents, her kids, her nieces and nephews. She knows this life because she lived it.

At 26, a sudden health crisis left her in a wheelchair, facing the possibility she might never walk again. When doctors couldn’t explain it, they offered little hope, and for a while she almost believed them. Then something deep within whispered, don’t give up. Deep in her heart, she knew this wasn’t how her story was going to end.

“The experiences we wish never happened often become the very ones that reveal what matters most.”
