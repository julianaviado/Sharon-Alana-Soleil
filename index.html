How it works

Loving people shouldn’t cost you yourself.

There’s another way to live that doesn’t ask you to do more.

The pressure isn’t coming from them. It’s coming from you. If overthinking, overgiving, overachieving, or overextending have become your default way of moving through life, you are not alone—and you are likely experiencing Over Syndrome™, the internal pressure to do more, give more, and expect more of yourself until you lose sight of what you need and what matters most.

Overthinking

You replay every conversation, and can’t turn your brain off.

Overgiving

You give until you’re naubos: poured out, nothing left for you.

Overachieving

Nothing ever feels like enough, no matter what you accomplish.

Overextending

You say yes before you’ve asked yourself whether you can.

Over-independent

You’d rather do it all yourself than risk needing anyone.
